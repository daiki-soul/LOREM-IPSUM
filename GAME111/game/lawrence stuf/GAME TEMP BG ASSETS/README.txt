I tried to name the images in a way that makes them easy to understand (in an [image name]_[time of day]_[weather] format). Here's a quick guide as to what they mean:
* TIME OF DAY:
** default (no variation name): set in the day
** aft: set in the afternoon (more orange lighting)
** night: set at night
** nolight: like night, but certain elements are changed to not give off light

WEATHER:
* cloudy: sky and/or lighting is changed to look cloudy
* stormy: has dark clouds and wet surfaces
* rainy: like stormy, but with rain

Additionally, some backgrounds have variations specific to itself. The variations will be part of the image name (e.g. "poolempty_night"). These images are:
* beach: "house" variation adds a house to the background
* bedroom2: "empty" variation removes all furniture, "box" variation is like "empty" but with boxes
* building: "text" variation adds text to the top of the building
* classroom3: "empty" variation removes all furniture
* classroom5: "empty" variation removes all furniture, "art" variation is like "empty" but with easels and a table
* infirmary: "curtains" variation closes the curtains on the rightmost bed
* livingroom2: "items" variation adds various items to the foreground
* pool: "drained" variation removes the water from the pool
* sky: "clear" variation has no noticeable clouds
* street2: "icy" variation has snow everywhere, "snowy" variation is like "icy" but with snowflakes

All images not in "other" or "multi-layered" have day, afternoon, and night variations.
